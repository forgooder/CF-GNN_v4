## 9. Analysis

Negative diagnostic. The shortcut penalty does not solve the WN18RR_v1 gap and appears to worsen mask decomposition: shortcut raw masks become high, shortcut entropy stays low, and overlap becomes large. Validation AUC-PR is weaker than the current no-penalty WN18RR_v1 diagnostics.

This suggests that direct shortcut score suppression is not a useful structural direction for WN18RR under the current shared-scorer setup. The WN18RR problem is more likely that the auxiliary masked-score training distorts or fails to improve the original scorer, rather than that shortcut score separation alone needs a penalty.

## 10. Next steps

1. Do not test or extend this shortcut-penalty configuration.
2. Avoid larger shortcut penalty sweeps unless there is a redesigned objective, because the first diagnostic moved mask overlap in the wrong direction.
3. Next WN18RR direction should be a true structural change, such as separating gradients from auxiliary masked losses, or a validation-only check with a much weaker/no auxiliary masked loss to quantify how much the original scorer is being distorted.
