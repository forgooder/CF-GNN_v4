## 8. Diagnosis

Alpha-specific causal-only regularization is effective for mask health on WN18RR_v1. It prevents the severe late shortcut collapse and hard alpha saturation seen in earlier v5 effect-loss runs.

The remaining problem is performance, not mask collapse. With effect loss disabled, `causal_plus_effect` is not trained to become a strong scoring mode, and the model no longer recovers the v4 WN18RR gains. The original and causal scores also show moderate magnitude drift late in training, but without the catastrophic explosions seen when effect loss is enabled.
