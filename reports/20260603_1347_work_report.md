## Conclusion

Negative diagnostic. Linear effect-loss ramp does not fix collapse. Alpha becomes unstable and low-entropy during warmup, and shortcut still collapses near zero at the first ramp epoch. By epoch15 the model is in a high-budget, high-overlap, low-entropy mask state.

## Next Action

Stop scalar-only WN18RR_v2 tuning. The next implementation should target the mask dynamics directly, likely through staged mask regularization or stop-gradient isolation between alpha/beta/effect paths.
