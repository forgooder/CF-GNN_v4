## Conclusion

Negative diagnostic. Lowering `effect_loss_weight` from 0.1 to 0.02 prevents immediate beta raw collapse to zero, but it does not produce healthy masks. The new failure mode is high-overlap, low-entropy masks with a large budget loss.

This suggests the next change should be structural rather than another isolated scalar sweep. A gradual effect-loss ramp or staged mask regularization is the best next candidate before any more full test evaluations.
