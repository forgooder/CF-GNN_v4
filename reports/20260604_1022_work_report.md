## 6. Conclusion

Lowering `causal_loss_weight` from 1.0 to 0.5 or 0.3 does not solve the WN18RR_v1 validation gap. Both settings keep masks mostly healthy, but validation AUC-PR remains around 0.919, well below the same-environment baseline 0.9350.

This suggests the current v5 alpha-specific regularized causal-only design is no longer failing primarily through mask collapse on WN18RR_v1. The limiting factor is the scoring/training objective: healthy masks are not translating into stronger relation prediction.
