## 9. Conclusion

Negative formal result.

The all-mode diagnostic was useful: it showed the `original` scorer can be much stronger than causal/effect modes under causal training, and it produced one validation checkpoint slightly above the same-environment AUC-PR baseline. However, the validation signal was unstable and did not transfer to test. Test AUC-PR was 0.9221, and Hits@10 was 0.7712, both far below the same-environment GRAIL baseline.

Mask health was acceptable, so this is not a mask-collapse failure. The current v5 training objective can produce healthy masks but does not yet create stable generalization gains on WN18RR_v1.
