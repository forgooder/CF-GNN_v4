## Conclusion

This is a negative diagnostic result. The entropy floor regularizer runs and logs correctly, but weight `0.05` does not prevent mask saturation. It also does not improve metrics:

- Best AUC-PR `0.9244` is below same-environment baseline `0.9350`.
- Hits@10 `0.7735` is below same-environment baseline `0.8404`.
- Alpha collapse persists; beta/shortcut raw trends low late in training.

Next candidate should not simply repeat this configuration. A stronger fix likely needs an explicit mask-temperature schedule, delayed hardening, stronger entropy/anti-saturation pressure on mask logits, or a different validation/regularization schedule that does not allow epoch-1 selection while later masks collapse.
