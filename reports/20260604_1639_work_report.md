## 9. Analysis

Negative diagnostic. Mask-only auxiliary gradients keep masks healthy but do not close the WN18RR_v1 validation gap. This weakens the hypothesis that WN18RR failure is mainly caused by masked auxiliary losses distorting the shared GNN/scorer.

Compared with the no-penalty original-score mainline, this run is still weaker on validation AUC-PR. The structural change may be useful as a diagnostic tool, but it is not a WN18RR solution by itself.
