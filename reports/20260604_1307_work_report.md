## 9. Analysis

The repeat strengthens the FB237_v1 conclusion. The first validation point was weak, but later validation recovered to AUC-PR 0.8592, 0.8578, and best 0.8683, all above the same-env baseline. This suggests the previous formal FB237_v1 success was not only a single-run validation artifact.

The shortcut diagnostic score is consistently strong in this repeat. That does not invalidate the original-score formal result, but it suggests that the current mask split may behave more like a robust ensemble/regularizer on FB237_v1 than a clean causal/shortcut separation.
