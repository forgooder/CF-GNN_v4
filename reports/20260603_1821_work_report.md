This is not eligible for test evaluation. The validation score improves over the smoke, but mask health is mixed: beta remains healthy, while alpha drifts open and low-entropy by epoch10. This repeats the project-level pattern that alpha collapse/hardening can happen even when beta is protected and the effect objective is disabled.
